# Partnership Outreach — DRAFTS (do not send without approval)

## Targets (find 5 of each within 15 miles of your base)
1. Self-storage facilities (Public Storage, Extra Space, CubeSmart, Life Storage, local)
2. U-Haul neighborhood dealers + Penske/Budget counters
3. Apartment leasing offices (large complexes, student housing)
4. Real-estate agents / property managers (Facebook realtor groups)
5. Furniture/mattress stores that deliver curbside

## Walk-in / phone script (storage & truck counters) — 20 seconds
```
Hi, I'm [NAME], I do moving labor around here — loading and unloading trucks and storage units. When customers ask you if you know anyone who can help carry, could you hand them this card? Text or WhatsApp works best: 305-781-5530. Happy to be on your list.
```
Leave 10 simple cards (name, "Moving Help – Load/Unload/Storage", phone, "Text first").

```

[YOUR NAME]

```
Hi [NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME], an independent moving helper covering Miami-Dade/Broward/Palm Beach. If a client needs help loading a truck, moving furniture or clearing a storage unit before closing, I can usually fit same-week. Text/WhatsApp 305-781-5530. Happy to help your clients — [YOUR NAME]

## Referral offer (optional)
"$20 thank-you for each booked job you send me" — only if you want to; many partners prefer no money, just reliability.

## Follow-up process
1. Day 0: send message / drop cards. Log in `04-lead-tracker/leads.xlsx` → "Campaign Log" (Channel = Partner).
2. Day 7: one follow-up ("Just checking in — still happy to help your customers").
3. Day 30: short update ("Just did two moves for [area] residents — thank you").
4. After every referred job: thank the partner the same day.
