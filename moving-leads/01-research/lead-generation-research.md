# South Florida Moving Labor — Lead Generation Research
Date: 2026-10-17 | Business phone: 305-781-5530 | Contact preference: text / WhatsApp first

## 1. Bottom line (read this first)
1. **Highest-intent, lowest-cost channel: Craigslist `services > labor / moving`** on the South Florida site (miami.craigslist.org). Services posts cost **$5 per 30 days** (craigslist posting-fee page). People searching there are ready to book.
2. **Second: Facebook** — Marketplace "Home & garden services"-style listings, local buy/sell + apartment groups, and a $1–$5/day ad pointed at WhatsApp. Free posting first, paid test second.
3. **Third: marketplaces that already have the customers** — U-Haul Moving Help® and TaskRabbit list thousands of Miami labor providers; they bring jobs without ads but take a cut and require sign-up / background check. Worth joining in parallel; they do not need the $20.
4. **Legal flag (decision required):** Florida Statute 507.01(9) defines a *mover* as anyone who, for compensation, engages in "the loading, transportation or shipment, **or unloading** of household goods as part of a household move." There is **no explicit labor-only exemption**. Unregistered movers face cease-and-desist orders and fines up to $5,000. Broward and Miami-Dade also run county mover registrations. See §6 before publishing.

## 3. Channel-by-channel findings

### A. Craigslist (priority #1)
- Site: `miami.craigslist.org` with sub-areas **mia** (Miami-Dade), **brw** (Broward), **pbc** (Palm Beach).
- Category: `services > labor / hauling / moving` (listed as "labor/move"). Fee: $5 (US services). Gigs category is $3–$10 but is for hiring people, not offering services.
- Rules that get accounts banned: posting the same ad in more than one sub-area or category, reposting while a live ad exists, keyword stuffing, fake locations. **One live ad, one sub-area, one category.** Renew/repost only after it expires or you delete it.
- Pick the sub-area where you are based (travel distance matters). Default recommendation: **Broward (brw)** if you are central; it borders both Miami-Dade and Palm Beach. Change to `mia` if you are south of Miami.

### B. Facebook (priority #2)
- **Free:** post in local groups (buy/sell, "Moving sale", apartment-community, neighborhood groups) and Marketplace. Most groups allow service posts only on designated days or require admin approval — read each group's rules before posting.
- **Business Page:** worth creating (free, 10 minutes). Needed for ads, for a Marketplace services presence, and for the WhatsApp click-to-message ad.
- **Paid:** Meta accepts $1/day budgets; practical floor ~$5/day. A "Send WhatsApp message" objective ad, radius-targeted, is the most direct path to a text conversation. Expect only 2–8 conversations from $10; it is a test, not a campaign.

### C. Google Business Profile (free, slower payoff)
- A service-area business with no storefront **is eligible**; you hide the address and list service areas (Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach). Google still requires a real address behind the profile (home address is normal) for verification. Verification may require video.
- Category suggestion: "Moving and storage service" or "Mover" — **only if you resolve the registration question**; otherwise use "Labor" / "Handyman"-type categories cautiously. Do not invent an office.

### D. Nextdoor (free)
- Business pages are free; neighbors can recommend you. Service providers without a formal business may need documents to post to the feed. Local deals start around $3. Good for repeat/referral traffic; low effort to set up.

### E. Marketplaces (free to join, commission on jobs)
- **U-Haul Moving Help®** — customers renting trucks book labor there. Provider app "Moving Helper". Application + review. No ad spend needed. Highest fit for "load/unload the U-Haul" jobs.
- **TaskRabbit** — background check, set your own rate; Miami is crowded but busy.
- **HireAHelper** — fee-heavy for small jobs; lower priority.

### F. Partnerships (free, needs your approval to send)
- Self-storage facilities (Public Storage, Extra Space, CubeSmart, local ones) — managers get asked "do you know anyone to help unload?" daily.
- U-Haul neighborhood dealers and truck-rental counters.
- Apartment leasing offices (move-in days) and property managers.
- Real estate agents / Facebook realtor groups.
- Templates in `07-templates/partnership-outreach.md`. **Nothing is sent until you approve.**

## 4. Demand timing
- Weekends and month-end (28th–3rd) are peak for apartment moves. Post/renew Craigslist on Thursday so it is fresh for weekend searches.
- Snowbird season (Oct–Apr) and lease cycles (Aug, Dec/Jan) raise demand.

## 6. Compliance notes (verify before advertising "moving")
- **Florida Ch. 507**: mover = loads/transports/unloads household goods for compensation. Registration with FDACS ≈ $300/yr + proof of insurance; each ad must show "Fla. Mover Reg. No. ___". Penalty up to $5,000.
- **Miami-Dade**: movers doing local moves must also register with the county (Consumer Protection, 786-469-2300, license@miamidade.gov).
- **Broward**: county registration ($325 + $50 vehicle decal per Broward's page).
- **What this means for you:** Loading/unloading a customer's own U-Haul for a home-to-home move arguably fits the statute's "mover" definition even without a truck. Many labor-only helpers operate via U-Haul Moving Help/TaskRabbit anyway, but the risk is yours. Recommended path:
  - **Option 1 (safest):** Call FDACS at 1-800-HELP-FLA (435-7352) and ask whether labor-only loading/unloading requires registration. 10-minute call. Decide after.
  - **Option 2:** Launch now with services that are clearly outside "household move" scope — furniture rearranging inside a home, storage-unit organizing, heavy-item lifting, junk/garage help — and add load/unload after confirming.
  - **Option 3:** Register (≈$300 + insurance) and advertise freely as a labor-only mover.
- All ad copy in this package: no "licensed/insured/bonded", no "moving company", no truck offered unless you confirm you have one.
