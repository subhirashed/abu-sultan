# Nextdoor, Google Business Profile & Community Boards

## Nextdoor (free)
1. nextdoor.com → "For business" → claim/create Business Page. Category: Moving & Storage / Labor. Service area: Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach.
2. Ask the first 3 satisfied customers to leave a Nextdoor recommendation (template in 07-templates).
3. Post (only where the neighborhood allows business posts, or via "Local Deals" ~ $3):
```
Hi neighbors — I'm not a moving company, I'm a person who worked in moving for years and does it the simple way: I bring the U-Haul, my team and the equipment, we load, drive, unload and reassemble your furniture, and we agree on one price together. Text or WhatsApp 305-781-5530 with your date and a few photos.
```

## Google Business Profile (free; set up after you decide on the registration question)
- business.google.com → Add business → "I deliver goods and services to my customers" (service-area business) → hide address.
- Name: South Florida Moving Help. Phone: 305-781-5530. Hours: e.g., Mon–Sun 7am–9pm (set what is true).
- Service areas: Miami, Hialeah, Hollywood, Fort Lauderdale, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Boca Raton, West Palm Beach.
- Description (750 chars max):
```
Independent moving help across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach. I'm a person who worked in moving for years, not a company. I bring the truck, my team and the equipment; we load, transport, unload, and take apart and reassemble furniture. One price agreed together after seeing your items. Text or WhatsApp 305-781-5530.
```
- Do **not** add a storefront, fake reviews, or "licensed & insured" unless true. Verification may require a short video showing equipment and proof of location.
